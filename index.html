Welcome to NEXA 🎉

Everything you need to hit the ground running. Complete your Day 1 checklist, meet your team, and get fully set up in your first week.

✅ Day 1 Setup Checklist
✓
Connect to WiFi & sign into laptopSign in via personal Microsoft account on first boot
✓
Set up Gmail — firstname@digitalnexa.comSend a test email to the Operations Manager before emailing any client
✓
Download Slack & sign in with NEXA credentialsJoin #general, #your-department, and #nexa-social on Day 1
✓
Access Google Drivedrive.google.com — NEVER save client files locally
Log into People HR & complete your profileAll leave requests go through People HR — not Slack or email
Enable 2-Factor Authentication on all accountsGmail, Google Drive, Slack, HubSpot — mandatory from Day 1
Set up your email signatureName, title, NEXA logo, phone, address, wearenexa.com — get logo from Ops
Read this handbook end to endYes, the whole thing. It'll save you a lot of "how do I…" questions later.
📅 Your First Week
Day 1
Arrive & Set Up
Complete IT setup, access all tools, meet your team and manager.
Day 2
Learn the Brand
Study the NEXA Brand Guidelines, AM Handbook and Google Drive structure.
Day 3
Shadow Your Team
Sit in on stand-ups and client calls. Understand current accounts and projects.
Day 4
Configure Your Tools
Set up Forecast for time tracking, confirm HubSpot access, complete People HR profile.
Day 5
Set Your 30-Day Goals
First 1:1 with your manager. Agree on priorities, success metrics and your learning plan.
